    <div class="bp__article">
      <p class="ip-p bp__p">For years, the fastest way to shortlist a large applicant pool was a GPA cutoff. It's simple, defensible, and easy to apply at scale — and it's also a weak signal for whether someone can actually do the job.</p>
      <div class="bp__stats">
        <div><div class="bp__stat-n">30–40%</div><div class="bp__stat-l">faster screening with JD-matched shortlists</div></div>

        <div><div class="bp__stat-n">1 score</div><div class="bp__stat-l">recruiters now ask for, alongside the resume</div></div>
      </div>
      <h2 class="ip-h2" id="gpa-misses">What GPA misses</h2>
      <p class="ip-p bp__p">A strong GPA correlates with discipline, but it doesn't measure communication under pressure, structured problem-solving, or how close a candidate's actual skillset is to a specific job description. We've seen students with modest GPAs outperform toppers in technical interviews simply because their prep was targeted, not broad.</p>
      <h2 class="ip-h2" id="instead">What recruiters are asking for instead</h2>
      <p class="ip-p bp__p">The recruiters we work with increasingly ask placement cells for a readiness score matched against the JD — not a transcript. It compresses screening time and, more importantly, surfaces candidates a GPA filter would have screened out entirely.</p>
      <ul class="bp__list">
        <li>Recruiters using JD-matched shortlists report screening interviews complete 30–40% faster on average.</li>
        <li>Shortlists built on readiness scores show more consistent interview performance than GPA-filtered shortlists, because the score already accounts for communication and aptitude, not just academic record.</li>
        <li>Several recruiting partners now request the readiness breakdown alongside the resume, not as a replacement for it.</li>
      </ul>
      <p class="ip-p bp__p">GPA isn't going away — it's still a useful baseline. But as a sole filter, it's increasingly seen as a blunt instrument for a problem that now has a more precise one.</p>
      <blockquote class="bp__quote">We used to lose good candidates to a 0.2-point GPA cutoff. Now we look at readiness against the actual JD, and the quality of our shortlist went up, not down. — Campus Recruiting Lead, partner company</blockquote>
