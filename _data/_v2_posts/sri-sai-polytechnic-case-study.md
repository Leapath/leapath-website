<section class="ip-section">
  <div class="container cs-article">
    <p>Diploma students at Sri Sai Polytechnic were consistently losing out to degree-holders in mixed campus drives — not on aptitude, but on structured, JD-aligned preparation that most polytechnics simply didn't have the staff to build in-house.</p>
    <div class="cs-quote" style="padding:2.5rem 0">
      <div class="cs-quote__mark">"</div>
      <div class="cs-quote__t">We didn't need more lectures. We needed to know exactly which 15% of the syllabus each student was weak on, three months before the drive.</div>
      <div class="cs-quote__by">Training & Placement Officer, Sri Sai Polytechnic</div>
    </div>
    <div class="cs-steps">
      <div class="cs-step">
        <div class="cs-step__ico"><svg class="icon" aria-hidden="true"><use href="#ic-target"></use></svg></div>
        <div>
          <div class="cs-step__label">Challenge</div>
          <div class="cs-step__t">Diploma students under-prepared for mixed campus drives</div>
          <div class="cs-step__d">Generic training calendars treated every student the same, so strong students were bored and weak students never caught up before recruiters arrived.</div>
        </div>
      </div><div class="cs-step">
        <div class="cs-step__ico"><svg class="icon" aria-hidden="true"><use href="#ic-settings"></use></svg></div>
        <div>
          <div class="cs-step__label">Approach</div>
          <div class="cs-step__t">Individual skill-gap roadmaps for every student</div>
          <div class="cs-step__d">Each student's assessment results generated a personal learning path — resequencing the same faculty hours around what each student actually needed to close.</div>
        </div>
      </div><div class="cs-step">
        <div class="cs-step__ico"><svg class="icon" aria-hidden="true"><use href="#ic-trending-up"></use></svg></div>
        <div>
          <div class="cs-step__label">Result</div>
          <div class="cs-step__t">Placement rate nearly doubled in two seasons</div>
          <div class="cs-step__d">From 52% to 81% over two consecutive placement seasons, with employer visits tripling as recruiters saw a more consistent standard of readiness across the batch.</div>
        </div>
      </div>
