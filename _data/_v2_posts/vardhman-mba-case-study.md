<section class="ip-section">
  <div class="container cs-layout">
    <div class="cs-article">
    <p>This Career Services team had a quiet but expensive problem: employers were confirming campus visits, then quietly no-showing or sending a junior representative once they saw the actual shortlist wasn't matched to the role they'd shared.</p>
    <div class="cs-quote" style="padding:2.5rem 0">
      <div class="cs-quote__mark">"</div>
      <div class="cs-quote__t">Employers don't ghost you because they're busy. They ghost you because the last shortlist wasted their day. Fix that, and they show up early.</div>
      <div class="cs-quote__by">Career Services Coordinator</div>
    </div>
    <div class="cs-steps">
      <div class="cs-step">
        <div class="cs-step__ico"><svg class="icon" aria-hidden="true"><use href="#ic-target"></use></svg></div>
        <div>
          <div class="cs-step__label">Challenge</div>
          <div class="cs-step__t">Shortlists looked strong on paper, weak on the actual role</div>
          <div class="cs-step__d">Resumes were filtered by GPA and elective choices, not by the specific skills an employer's role actually asked for, so first-round match rates were low.</div>
        </div>
      </div>
      <div class="cs-step">
        <div class="cs-step__ico"><svg class="icon" aria-hidden="true"><use href="#ic-settings"></use></svg></div>
        <div>
          <div class="cs-step__label">Approach</div>
          <div class="cs-step__t">Role-to-skill matching before every shortlist goes out</div>
          <div class="cs-step__d">Each role was run against live readiness and skill-gap data before a single profile was shared, so employers only ever saw candidates matched on the criteria they'd actually asked for.</div>
        </div>
      </div>
      <div class="cs-step">
        <div class="cs-step__ico"><svg class="icon" aria-hidden="true"><use href="#ic-trending-up"></use></svg></div>
        <div>
          <div class="cs-step__label">Result</div>
          <div class="cs-step__t">Employers started showing up, and staying longer</div>
          <div class="cs-step__d">No-shows dropped 40% in two hiring cycles, and the shortlist-to-offer ratio more than doubled as employers spent their day meeting candidates worth meeting.</div>
        </div>
      </div>
    </div>
