<section class="ip-section">
  <div class="container cs-article">
    <p>The placement cell had one part-time officer and no budget for outside training vendors — while private colleges nearby ran fully staffed placement departments with dedicated trainers for every skill track.</p>
    <div class="cs-quote" style="padding:2.5rem 0">
      <div class="cs-quote__mark">"</div>
      <div class="cs-quote__t">We were never going to out-spend the private colleges. We just needed to stop guessing which students needed help, and use the one officer we had where it mattered.</div>
      <div class="cs-quote__by">Placement Officer, Government Engineering College, Nagpur</div>
    </div>
    <div class="cs-steps">
      <div class="cs-step">
        <div class="cs-step__ico"><svg class="icon" aria-hidden="true"><use href="#ic-target"></use></svg></div>
        <div>
          <div class="cs-step__label">Challenge</div>
          <div class="cs-step__t">One officer, no vendor budget, a widening gap</div>
          <div class="cs-step__d">With no funds for external trainers, the placement cell's only lever was faculty time — and faculty time was being spread evenly across a batch with very uneven needs.</div>
        </div>
      </div><div class="cs-step">
        <div class="cs-step__ico"><svg class="icon" aria-hidden="true"><use href="#ic-settings"></use></svg></div>
        <div>
          <div class="cs-step__label">Approach</div>
          <div class="cs-step__t">Free assessments, gap-targeted faculty hours</div>
          <div class="cs-step__d">Readiness assessments replaced guesswork, and the placement officer used faculty free periods to run small-group sessions targeted only at students flagged as at-risk that week.</div>
        </div>
      </div><div class="cs-step">
        <div class="cs-step__ico"><svg class="icon" aria-hidden="true"><use href="#ic-trending-up"></use></svg></div>
        <div>
          <div class="cs-step__label">Result</div>
          <div class="cs-step__t">More than double the offers, same rupee spent</div>
          <div class="cs-step__d">Offers more than doubled year-over-year for the eligible batch, entirely through better-targeted use of existing faculty time — with no new hires and no external spend.</div>
        </div>
      </div>
    </div>
