  <div class="container cs-layout">
    <div class="cs-article">
    <p>The Career Services team had one staff member and no budget for outside training vendors, while larger institutions nearby ran fully staffed Career Services departments with dedicated trainers for every skill track.</p>
    <div class="cs-quote" style="padding:2.5rem 0">
      <div class="cs-quote__mark">"</div>
      <div class="cs-quote__t">They were never going to out-spend the bigger institutions. They just needed to stop guessing which students needed help, and use the one person they had where it mattered.</div>
      <div class="cs-quote__by">Career Services Officer</div>
    </div>
    <div class="cs-steps">
      <div class="cs-step">
        <div class="cs-step__ico"><svg class="icon" aria-hidden="true"><use href="#ic-target"></use></svg></div>
        <div>
          <div class="cs-step__label">Challenge</div>
          <div class="cs-step__t">One staff member, no vendor budget, a widening gap</div>
          <div class="cs-step__d">With no funds for external trainers, the Career Services team's only lever was staff time, and that time was being spread evenly across a cohort with very uneven needs.</div>
        </div>
      </div>
      <div class="cs-step">
        <div class="cs-step__ico"><svg class="icon" aria-hidden="true"><use href="#ic-settings"></use></svg></div>
        <div>
          <div class="cs-step__label">Approach</div>
          <div class="cs-step__t">Free assessments, gap-targeted staff hours</div>
          <div class="cs-step__d">Readiness assessments replaced guesswork, and the Career Services team used available hours to run small-group sessions targeted only at students flagged as at-risk that week.</div>
        </div>
      </div>
      <div class="cs-step">
        <div class="cs-step__ico"><svg class="icon" aria-hidden="true"><use href="#ic-trending-up"></use></svg></div>
        <div>
          <div class="cs-step__label">Result</div>
          <div class="cs-step__t">More than double the offers, same spend</div>
          <div class="cs-step__d">Offers more than doubled year-over-year for the eligible cohort, entirely through better-targeted use of existing staff time, with no new hires and no external spend.</div>
        </div>
      </div>
