    <p>Each of Nexus's five campuses ran placements independently — different assessments, different definitions of "ready," and a central office that only found out a campus was behind once recruiters started asking questions.</p>
    <div class="cs-quote" style="padding:2.5rem 0">
      <div class="cs-quote__mark">"</div>
      <div class="cs-quote__t">Before this, 'readiness' meant five different things depending on which campus you asked. Now it means one number, everywhere.</div>
      <div class="cs-quote__by">Group Director of Placements, Nexus University Network</div>
    </div>
    <div class="cs-steps">
      <div class="cs-step">
        <div class="cs-step__ico"><svg class="icon" aria-hidden="true"><use href="#ic-target"></use></svg></div>
        <div>
          <div class="cs-step__label">Challenge</div>
          <div class="cs-step__t">Five campuses, five definitions of "placement ready"</div>
          <div class="cs-step__d">Central leadership had no reliable, comparable view of which campuses were on track and which needed intervention until results came in at season's end.</div>
        </div>
      </div><div class="cs-step">
        <div class="cs-step__ico"><svg class="icon" aria-hidden="true"><use href="#ic-settings"></use></svg></div>
        <div>
          <div class="cs-step__label">Approach</div>
          <div class="cs-step__t">One readiness standard, rolled out network-wide</div>
          <div class="cs-step__d">A shared assessment framework and scoring model went live across all five campuses simultaneously, with a group-level dashboard rolling up every campus into one view.</div>
        </div>
      </div><div class="cs-step">
        <div class="cs-step__ico"><svg class="icon" aria-hidden="true"><use href="#ic-trending-up"></use></svg></div>
        <div>
          <div class="cs-step__label">Result</div>
          <div class="cs-step__t">A single, comparable view — three months earlier</div>
          <div class="cs-step__d">Group leadership could now see which campuses and departments were falling behind by December instead of April, and redirect training resources network-wide before season opened.</div>
