  <div class="container cs-layout">
    <div class="cs-article">
    <p>Going into the 2025 hiring cycle, this Career Services team had a familiar problem: a strong top quartile of students with multiple offers, and a long tail of students who weren't getting shortlisted, without a clear, shared picture of why.</p>
    <div class="cs-quote" style="padding:2.5rem 0">
      <div class="cs-quote__mark">"</div>
      <div class="cs-quote__t">The real shift wasn't in what we taught students, it was in when we found out who needed help. That timing change was the whole story.</div>
      <div class="cs-quote__by">Career Services Director</div>
    </div>
    <div class="cs-steps">
      <div class="cs-step">
        <div class="cs-step__ico"><svg class="icon" aria-hidden="true"><use href="#ic-target"></use></svg></div>
        <div>
          <div class="cs-step__label">Challenge</div>
          <div class="cs-step__t">No shared picture of who was actually at risk</div>
          <div class="cs-step__d">A one-time pre-hiring test told the team where students stood in November, but told them nothing about how that changed week to week as hiring approached.</div>
        </div>
      </div>
      <div class="cs-step">
        <div class="cs-step__ico"><svg class="icon" aria-hidden="true"><use href="#ic-settings"></use></svg></div>
        <div>
          <div class="cs-step__label">Approach</div>
          <div class="cs-step__t">A weekly readiness review, three months out</div>
          <div class="cs-step__d">Every week, the Career Services team pulled a list of students whose live scores had dropped or stalled, and routed them into the relevant remediation track that same week, mock interviews, technical bootcamps, or communication drills.</div>
        </div>
      </div>
      <div class="cs-step">
        <div class="cs-step__ico"><svg class="icon" aria-hidden="true"><use href="#ic-trending-up"></use></svg></div>
        <div>
          <div class="cs-step__label">Result</div>
          <div class="cs-step__t">A 34% relative lift, in one hiring cycle</div>
          <div class="cs-step__d">Student job readiness rate rose from 61% to 82% year-over-year for the eligible cohort. At-risk students were flagged an average of 94 days before their first interview, up from roughly 18 days the previous cycle, and employers could see a readiness number per shortlist, not just a resume.</div>
        </div>
      </div>
