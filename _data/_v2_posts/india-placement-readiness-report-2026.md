      <p class="ip-p bp__p">Every placement season, the same story repeats: a small group of students gets multiple offers while a much larger group gets none — not because they lack ability, but because nobody flagged the gap in time. This report is our attempt to put numbers behind that pattern.</p>
      <p class="ip-p bp__p">Over the 2025–26 academic year, we tracked readiness scores for 2,500+ students across 40 partner institutions — engineering colleges, MBA programmes, and polytechnics spanning eight states. Every student was scored against the same rubric: communication, aptitude, domain depth, and resume-to-JD match.</p>
      <div class="bp__stats">
        <div><div class="bp__stat-n">2,500+</div><div class="bp__stat-l">students scored across 40 partner institutions</div></div>
        <div><div class="bp__stat-n">34%</div><div class="bp__stat-l">showed early warning signs 90+ days before their first interview</div></div>
        <div><div class="bp__stat-n">2.3×</div><div class="bp__stat-l">faster gap closure when flagged early vs. in the final 30 days</div></div>
      </div>
      <h2 class="ip-h2" id="headline">The headline number</h2>
      <p class="ip-p bp__p">34% of students who were eventually placed showed early warning signs — a readiness score below the cutoff — at least 90 days before their first interview. Of those, only 41% received any structured intervention from their placement cell before season opened.</p>
      <ul class="bp__list">
        <li>Students flagged 90+ days early and given a remediation plan closed the readiness gap 2.3x faster than those flagged in the final 30 days.</li>
        <li>Communication and aptitude — not technical depth — were the most common gap categories, present in 61% of at-risk profiles.</li>
        <li>Institutions running weekly readiness reviews placed 28% more students per batch than those reviewing once a semester.</li>
      </ul>
      <h2 class="ip-h2" id="tpos">What this means for TPOs</h2>
      <p class="ip-p bp__p">The data is consistent across institution tiers: the earlier a gap is visible, the cheaper it is to close. A 90-day runway turns a remediation plan into a few hours a week. A 30-day runway turns it into a crisis the week before recruiters arrive.</p>
      <p class="ip-p bp__p">We're publishing the full methodology and state-by-state breakdown alongside this summary so placement cells can benchmark their own batch against the national numbers. If you want to see where your batch stands, the readiness scoring used in this report is the same engine running inside Leapath today.</p>
      <blockquote class="bp__quote">The gap wasn't ability. It was visibility. By the time we could see who was at risk, there were three weeks left in the season — not enough to fix anything, only enough to panic. — TPO, Tier-1 engineering college, report respondent</blockquote>
