    <div class="bp__article">
      <p class="ip-p bp__p">Placement readiness is often discussed as a local problem — specific to one country's education system or job market. Once we had data from partner institutions across five countries, a different picture emerged: some patterns repeat everywhere, and a few are sharply local.</p>
      <div class="bp__stats">
        <div><div class="bp__stat-n">5</div><div class="bp__stat-l">countries compared on the same readiness rubric</div></div>
        <div><div class="bp__stat-n">#1 gap</div><div class="bp__stat-l">communication and structured problem-solving, in every market</div></div>
        <div><div class="bp__stat-n">60–90d</div><div class="bp__stat-l">early-flag runway needed, depending on season length</div></div>
      </div>
      <h2 class="ip-h2" id="universal">What's universal</h2>
      <p class="ip-p bp__p">In every country we studied, communication and structured problem-solving were the most common gap categories — more common than domain knowledge gaps, regardless of curriculum or language of instruction. Early flagging also helped everywhere: students identified as at-risk 90+ days out closed their gap faster than those flagged late, in every market.</p>
      <div class="bp__quote">The diagnostic travels well across borders. The remediation content on top of it has to flex by market.</div>
      <h2 class="ip-h2" id="not-universal">What's not universal</h2>
      <ul class="bp__list">
        <li>In markets with strong English-medium instruction, communication gaps were less about language and more about structure — answers were fluent but unfocused.</li>
        <li>Domain-depth gaps were more pronounced in institutions with limited access to updated industry curricula, particularly outside major metro areas.</li>
        <li>The size of the "early flag" advantage varied: in markets with shorter placement seasons, even a 60-day runway produced most of the benefit a 90-day runway gave elsewhere.</li>
      </ul>
      <h2 class="ip-h2" id="global">Why this matters for global institutions</h2>
      <p class="ip-p bp__p">For institutions operating across multiple countries, the temptation is to build one readiness programme and roll it out everywhere. Our data suggests the core diagnostic — score early, flag gaps by category — travels well. The remediation content on top of it needs to flex by market.</p>
