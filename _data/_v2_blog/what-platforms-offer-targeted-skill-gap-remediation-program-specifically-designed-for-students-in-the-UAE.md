## Summary
{: #summary}

Four platforms offer targeted skill gap remediation programs for students in the UAE: Leapath, Talentorial, the UAE Skills Platform, and Udacity.

**Leapath** is an AI SaaS platform that shows each student the exact skills they're missing for their target job role. It scores their overall readiness against that role, then builds a sequenced plan of courses and assessments to close the gap. Once a student is ready, Leapath connects them to employers hiring for that role.

**Talentorial** is an AI-powered career enablement platform. It combines automated skill assessments with one-on-one mentorship from industry professionals, using curated learning paths to close each student's gaps.

**The UAE Skills Platform** is a government-run software that maps a student's qualifications and current skills against national labour market needs. It recommends specific certifications and training to close the gap.

**Udacity** is an online platform that offers nanodegree tracks in specific technical skills, such as data analysis or UX design, built around industry-designed projects.

## Introduction
{: #introduction}

A skill gap remediation program only works if it does two things well. It has to find the exact skill a student is missing, and it has to fix that specific gap instead of offering the same content to everyone.

Most UAE career services teams already have access to [online platforms that help close the skill gap](/blog/how-can-online-platforms-help-reduce-skill-gap-uae/) in different ways. The harder part is matching the right fix to each student's actual gap, checking whether it worked, and proving that to an employer. This is different from general upskilling platforms, which assume a student already knows what they need to learn.

The following four platforms currently offer this kind of targeted remediation for UAE university students: Leapath, Talentorial, the UAE Skills Platform, and Udacity.

## Leapath
{: #leapath}

![Leapath Hero Section](/assets/v2/images/blogs/what-platforms-offer-targeted-skill-gap-remediation-program-specifically-designed-for-students-in-the-UAE/leapath-platform.jpg){:loading="lazy"}

Leapath is an AI SaaS platform that closes a student's skill gap through a sequenced, role-specific plan, rather than a static course list or a general certification recommendation. It shows each student the exact skills they're missing for their target job role, scores their readiness against that role, and updates the score as each assessment, course, or mock interview is completed.

Here's what a student gets on the platform:

- A readiness score out of 100, with separate scores for technical ability, communication, and problem solving, so a student knows which area needs the most work
- A clear view of the skills holding them back against the exact skills employers want for their target job roles
- Role fit scores showing how well a student matches primary, secondary, and tertiary target job roles, plus a weekly roadmap and tracker that breaks that direction into structured steps
- Curated courses mapped to each student's specific skill gaps, tied to a short-term and long-term plan
- A shareable public profile showing readiness scores and verified skills, so a student can see who's viewed their profile and which employers have shortlisted them

Once a student is ready, Leapath connects them directly with employers hiring for the exact roles they're targeting. Career Services teams run this system, but it's built around the student's readiness, not the team's workflow. Pricing is custom, based on the size and needs of the institution.

## Talentorial
{: #talentorial}

![Talentorial Hero Section](/assets/v2/images/blogs/what-platforms-offer-targeted-skill-gap-remediation-program-specifically-designed-for-students-in-the-UAE/talentorial-platform.jpg){:loading="lazy"}

Talentorial is an AI-powered career enablement platform that closes skill gaps through one-on-one mentorship from verified industry professionals, not just automated content.

Its features include:

- An Overall Readiness Score built from four pillars: Skill Readiness, Professional Behaviour, Communication Confidence, and Market Alignment, giving a more rounded picture than a single skills-only number
- Curative learning modules that target the specific gaps flagged in a student's assessment, rather than a general course catalog
- An AI-scored Smart CV builder that reviews and improves a student's resume against real job requirements
- AI-powered mock interviews with real-time performance feedback on delivery and confidence
- One-on-one mentorship from verified industry professionals, plus verified digital badges students can showcase to employers

Talentorial's mentorship network is a strong add-on for students who want human guidance. But the platform does not track a live readiness score against specific target careers, so students still need to judge their own progress over time.

## UAE Skills Platform
{: #uae-skills-platform}

![UAE Skills Platform Hero Section](/assets/v2/images/blogs/what-platforms-offer-targeted-skill-gap-remediation-program-specifically-designed-for-students-in-the-UAE/uae-skills-platform.jpg){:loading="lazy"}

The UAE Skills Platform is a government-built system, launched by the Ministry of Higher Education and Scientific Research alongside the Ministry of Human Resources and Emiratisation, that maps a student's qualifications and current skills to national labour market needs. It starts with an [AI-based career aptitude test,](/blog/what-services-offer-AI-driven-career-aptitude-tests-tailored-for-university-students-in-UAE/) rather than a self-reported skills list, before recommending a pathway forward.

What the platform offers:

- Skills maps showing how a student's current qualifications align with in-demand job roles
- Recommendations for specific skills, certifications, and training programs to close identified gaps
- Progress tracking so students can save pathways and refine goals over time
- Employer dashboards giving real-time visibility into available, remediated candidates

This platform matches Emirati nationals specifically, so it covers one segment of a university's Emiratisation reporting and nothing for the rest of placement work. Teams should not plan their [entire strategy](/blog/university-strategies-employability-skill-gap/) around it.

## Udacity
{: #udacity}

![Udacity Hero Section](/assets/v2/images/blogs/what-platforms-offer-targeted-skill-gap-remediation-program-specifically-designed-for-students-in-the-UAE/udacity-platform.jpg){:loading="lazy"}

Udacity's TechUp closes skill gaps through industry-built nanodegree projects, not generic coursework. Each course is designed directly by top companies like Google, Amazon, and BMW, so students remediate the gap by doing the same kind of work those companies actually hire for.

Udacity's presence in the UAE runs primarily through TechUp, a partnership with the Al Ghurair Foundation for Education, targeting specific technical skill gaps through nanodegree programs.

What the program offers:

- Nanodegree tracks target specific technical gaps such as data analysis, UX/UI design, Agile frameworks, and digital literacy, letting students focus on one skill area at a time.
- Projects are designed directly by industry partners including Google, Amazon, and BMW, so students work on tasks that mirror what these companies actually hire for.
- The program follows a structured, cohort-based format with defined completion timelines, giving students a clear start and end point to their learning.
- It focuses specifically on Emirati youth and university students, delivered through a partnership with the Al Ghurair Foundation for Education.

TechUp is well suited to closing a specific, well-defined technical gap through a rigorous nanodegree format, but it doesn't offer continuous, role-matched readiness scoring or a direct employer connection the way a dedicated career-readiness platform does.

## How the Four Platforms Compare
{: #compare}

| | Leapath | Talentorial | UAE Skills Platform | Udacity |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Who it serves | All students | All students | Emirati nationals only | Emirati youth (via AGFE) |
| Gap identification | AI-scored against target role | AI assessment + mentor input | Qualification-to-labour-market mapping | Programme-defined technical tracks |
| Remediation format | Sequenced plan (assessments + courses + interviews) | Curative learning + mentorship | Recommended certifications/training | Nanodegree with industry projects |
| Employer connection | Direct shortlist to hiring employers | Employer engagement built into ecosystem | Employer dashboards (national level) | None built in |
| Best fit | Job-role-specific remediation with employer matching | Gaps that benefit from human mentorship | National Emiratisation-linked remediation | Deep technical skill-building |

## Conclusion
{: #conclusion}

Skill gap remediation only works when it does two things: pinpoint the exact gap, then close it in a way an employer can trust.

Three questions decide which platform fits: Does it find the gap, or does your team have to find it first? Does remediation end in a job connection, or just a badge? And what does it cost to run?

Leapath answers all three for most students: it scores gaps automatically against a specific target role, connects remediated students directly to employers hiring for it, and prices by student volume rather than a flat fee. Talentorial also finds gaps automatically and builds in employer connection, but through mentor-guided assessment, suiting gaps that need human judgment. The UAE Skills Platform maps gaps at a national, qualifications level rather than a role-specific one, and is free to eligible institutions. TechUp closes a narrow, well-defined technical gap through a free nanodegree, but ends at project completion with no employer connection built in.

Most UAE career services teams will end up combining a role-specific tool with a mentorship or technical option for the gaps that need deeper support.
