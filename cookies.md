## 1. Introduction

This Cookie Policy explains how Leapath Technology ("**Leapath**", "we", "us", or "our") uses cookies and similar technologies on the Leapath Placement Intelligence Platform (the "**Platform**") accessible at [https://www.leapath.tech](https://www.leapath.tech). It should be read alongside our [Privacy Policy](/privacy-policy/), which explains how we handle personal data more broadly.

## 2. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They allow the Platform to recognise your browser, remember preferences, keep you signed in, and understand how the Platform is used. Similar technologies — such as local storage and session storage — serve comparable purposes and are covered by this Policy.

## 3. Types of Cookies We Use

<div class="legal-table-wrap" markdown="1">

| Category | Purpose | Can be disabled? |
|---|---|---|
| **Strictly necessary** | Authentication, session management, load balancing, and security. Required for the Platform to function. | No |
| **Functional** | Remembering preferences such as language, dashboard layout, and role-based views. | Yes, with reduced convenience |
| **Analytics** | Understanding feature usage and diagnosing errors, in aggregate or de-identified form where possible. | Yes |

</div>

We do not use cookies for third-party advertising or cross-site behavioural tracking.

## 4. Third-Party Cookies

Certain features of the Platform rely on service providers — such as infrastructure, analytics, or support tooling — that may set their own cookies subject to their respective policies. These providers act under the confidentiality and processing obligations described in our [Data Processing & Security Policy](/data-processing/).

## 5. Managing Your Preferences

Strictly necessary cookies cannot be disabled, as they are required for core functionality such as authentication. Functional and analytics cookies can be controlled through your browser settings, or through an in-product preference centre where available. Disabling optional cookies may limit certain features of the Platform.

Most browsers allow you to view, delete, and block cookies through their settings menu. Refer to your browser's help documentation for specific instructions.

## 6. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the technologies we use or applicable legal requirements. The "Last Updated" date at the top of this Policy indicates when it was most recently revised.
